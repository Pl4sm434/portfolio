The Basics

You are the health bar. Your hitbox height equals your current health - the lower your health, the smaller (and lighter) you are.
Lower weight means a higher jump. Health kits heal you but shrink the margin for error near lava.

Powerups

Dash - hold a direction + DASH to burst that way. Refills on landing.
Wall Jump - stick to a wall by holding toward it while falling, then JUMP off.
Anti-Gravity - DASH button while standing still flips gravity, letting you walk on ceilings.

Hazards

Lava drains health over time (hold JUMP to "swim" upward through it). Health Gates only open once your health passes a threshold. Shooters fire projectiles that cost 10 HP and grant brief invincibility.
